The Ideal

Renunciation and service — two currents of one river.

The Mission holds that every soul is potentially divine, and that the goal of life is to manifest that divinity. It is realized equally through inner discipline and through selfless work — worshipping God in the living human being. Four paths, one summit.

Karma — Work

Service to others as worship. The wavy waters in our emblem: activity performed without attachment to its fruit.

Bhakti — Devotion

The lotus of the heart, opening in love for the Divine. Faith, prayer, and surrender that soften and steady the mind.

Jnana — Knowledge

The rising sun of wisdom, discerning the real from the unreal, until the seeker knows the Self as one with all.

The Three Lights

The lives that lit the lamp.

A God-realized saint, the Holy Mother who embodied his message, and the monk who carried it to the world.

श्री
Prophet of the Age

Sri Ramakrishna

1836 — 1886 · Kamarpukur, Bengal

A priest at Dakshineswar who realized God through many paths — and taught that all religions are true roads to one reality: “Jato mat, tato path.”

माँ
The Holy Mother

Sri Sarada Devi

1853 — 1920 · Jayrambati, Bengal

Spiritual consort and first disciple of Sri Ramakrishna. Her boundless motherhood taught the world to see no one as a stranger.

स्वामी
The Wandering Monk

Swami Vivekananda

1863 — 1902 · Kolkata, Bengal

Chief disciple who electrified the 1893 Parliament of Religions in Chicago and founded the Ramakrishna Mission on 1 May 1897.
